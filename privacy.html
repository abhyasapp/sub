Privacy

Last updated: 2026-09-19

What we store

  • Account: username, password (stored only as a salted hash), name, e-mail, mobile number, and the sign-in method (password or Google).
  • Study data: your quiz progress, bookmarks, flags and wrong-answer list (cloud backup), weekly-set attempts and scores.
  • Written answers: PDFs you upload for subjective questions, plus marks and feedback.
  • Payments: transaction ID, remarks and the payment screenshot you upload, and whether it was approved.
  • Notifications: a push-notification token, only if you turn notifications on.
  • Activity log: administrative actions on your account (for example approvals), used to run the service.

Where it lives

Your data is kept in a Google Sheet and Google Drive folders owned by the person who runs Abhyas, and is processed by Google Apps Script. Only that person and the admins they appoint can open it. Sign-in with Google shares your name and verified e-mail with us. Page assets load from Google Fonts and public CDNs. We do not sell your data and show no ads.

Deleting your data

In the app, open Backup → Your data on the server. You can reset your progress everywhere, or delete your account. Deleting an account removes your login, progress, payment record, weekly-set attempts and written answers, moves the files you uploaded to the Drive trash (permanently removed by Google after about 30 days), and replaces your username in the report and activity lists with “[deleted user]”.

If you cannot log in, e-mail abhyasbymku@gmail.com from the address on your account and we will delete it for you.
