What Abhyas is

Practice for Nepal's Lok Sewa Aayog Level 7 Civil Engineering exam. Thousands of questions across the technical paper, plus General Knowledge practice that sits alongside it.

It keeps working when the power doesn't

Open a chapter once while you have signal and its questions are saved to your phone. After that it opens during load-shedding, on the bus, or anywhere with no connection at all. New chapters need a connection the first time.

It tells you what to revise next

Accuracy per chapter, a daily streak, and a miss list that brings questions back on a spacing schedule until you've got them right four times, days apart. Your progress follows you to any device you sign in on.

Written answers, marked by a person

A timed question every day and full 100-mark papers on demand. Write on paper, photograph it, upload one PDF, and an admin marks it and sends feedback.

Free to try first

Every new account gets a trial period with everything unlocked. Pay only once you know it's useful.

Common questions

What is Lok Sewa Aayog?

Nepal's Public Service Commission. It runs the civil service examinations, including the Level 7 Civil Engineering exam that Abhyas is built around.

Can I really use it with no internet?

Yes, for anything you've opened before. Once a chapter or subtopic has loaded while you were online, it stays on your device and opens with no connection. You can also download everything at once from the Downloads screen.

What does it cost?

New accounts start on a free trial with full access. After that there's a one-time payment of a small amount; the QR code is on the payment screen once your trial ends.

Is my progress safe if I change phone?

Yes. Progress backs up to your Abhyas account whenever you're online, and you can restore it on a new device by signing in. You can also keep a second copy in your own Google Drive, or export a backup file.

I forgot my password.

Use "Forgot your password?" on the sign-in form. If the username or email matches an account, a reset code goes to that account's email address.
